Less crowded
    
      
  • Two surfaces, not four columns: one table, and the detail in a drawer that takes the width when you need it.
    • 
      
  • The sidebar collapses to an icon rail (Collapse toggle above). Range, status codes and Scan sit in one header row.
    • 
      
  • Default view is Needs attention (High and Medium). The 3,936 Low alerts stay one click away.
    • 
    

    
Sources clear
    
      
  • One tab per source: health dot, High / Medium / Low counts, and its own state: last Scan time, scanning, or the reason it is down.
    • 
      
  • "All sources" is the first tab, then one per connected log source: Datadog, OpenTelemetry, CloudWatch, Loki, New Relic. Tabs scroll sideways when they do not fit.
    • 
      
  • An Alert belongs to one source: its row shows that source's logo, and the drawer names it.
    • 
    

    
Drawer
    
      
  • Header: severity, sources, Route, Service, env. Tabs: Overview, Samples, Query, History.
    • 
      
  • Overview leads with the four-step strip and the link-code form. A pinned footer holds the one primary action.
    • 
      
  • Query tab shows the Alert's source query in that source's syntax. Esc, the close button or the scrim closes the drawer.
