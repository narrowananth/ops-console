## What it is

A local web app that runs on your Mac and drives a coding agent. It can connect to services you sign in to, including Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Drive, so that the agent can use them for you.

## Your responsibilities

- You decide what the agent may do. Each Session's Access mode controls whether it asks before it acts, and in the most permissive mode it may edit Google Docs and Google Sheets you can open without asking. Review what the agent is about to do.
- Keep your Mac and your Google account secure. Anyone who uses your Mac can use what Ops Console is connected to.
- Follow the terms of the services you connect, including [Google's Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms), and of the AI provider whose account the agent runs on.
- Back up files you care about. The agent can change a Google Doc or Sheet you connected, and Google's version history is the way back.
