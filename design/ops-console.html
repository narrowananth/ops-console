Account switching

How the console moves sessions between your accounts.

Auto-switch accounts
When the active account reaches 90% of its usage window, the session moves to the next account with headroom. Context carries over.
Smart usage
Uses the account whose weekly quota would otherwise go unused first: weekly % left ÷ days until weekly reset. Accounts with a full 5-hour window are skipped.
sam@example.com is the smart pick: 16% of its weekly quota would go unused in 1 day. It is used automatically; turn Smart usage off to stop that.
